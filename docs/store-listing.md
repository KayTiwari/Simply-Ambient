## Full description

Simply Ambient is a calm, private space for sound and breath. Shape your own binaural frequency or start from a familiar brainwave band, layer in an offline soundscape, and let the sleep timer bring the room back to stillness. When you want structure, guided breathing, chakra tones, listening paths, and thoughtful daily practices are one tap away.

**Tones and audio**

• Choose a target binaural Beat and Carrier, or tune the left and right ears directly from 50 to 500 Hz
• Explore Delta, Theta, Schumann, Alpha, Beta, and Gamma starting points
• Use Solfeggio-inspired and traditional tuning references from 111 to 963 Hz
• Adjust tone volume independently and save custom frequency combinations
• Layer a binaural tone, a built-in soundscape, and your own audio file
• Choose a preset or custom sleep timer

**Thirteen offline soundscapes**

• Soft Rain, Ocean Tide, Forest Air, Trickling Stream, and Hearth
• White, Pink, and Brown Noise
• Night Breeze, Summer Night, Distant Thunder, Airplane Cabin, and Night Train
• Independent volume and animated atmosphere that matches the active scene

**Listening paths**

• Morning Focus, Evening Wind-down, and Deep Sleep
• Each path plays its frequencies in order and advances automatically
• The miniplayer shows the current step and time remaining

**Guided breathing**

• Eighteen calming and activating breathing rhythms
• Circle or layered lotus-mandala visuals
• Endless, 5, 10, or 20-cycle sessions
• Optional phase tones for practicing with your eyes closed
• Suggested hand positions and careful safety guidance

**Chakras and Stars**

• Seven chakra sigils with bija references, elements, body locations, and associated tones
• Vata, Pitta, and Kapha reference cards
• Live Daily, Weekly, and Monthly horoscope readings for all 12 signs
• Lunar illumination plus a countdown to the next Full or New Moon
• Upright and reversed tarot with Major or All deck choices and 3, 5, or 7-card spreads

**Private daily reflection**

• Mood check-ins, Gratitude, Release the Noise, Intentions, and 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding
• Daily affirmations with optional local notifications
• Gratitude reminders with a custom evening time
• Optional AI Insights using your own Gemini key, visible journal-source controls, and orientation-aware tarot interpretation
• Pin eligible tools to a horizontally scrollable navbar for faster access

**Made to be trusted**

No account. No ads. Journals and settings stay on your device unless you tap an AI Insights action with sources enabled. Tarot interpretation sends the drawn card name, orientation, matching meaning, and description. Filtered anonymous crash diagnostics help fix bugs without sending journal content or saved keys. Live horoscope, tarot, AI, and feedback features use the network only when needed. Read the full privacy policy for the complete breakdown.

Use stereo headphones to perceive the binaural difference. Simply Ambient is a wellness and reflection tool, not medical treatment.
