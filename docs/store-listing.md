## Long description / full description

Design your own personal binaural frequency, or explore the most practiced ones. Simply Ambient pairs custom binaural beats with guided breath work and a thoughtful set of grounded daily practices.

**Frequencies**
• Independent left & right ear sliders, 50 to 500 Hz
• Live audio that updates as you slide — no overlap
• Brainwave-band presets: Delta, Theta, Schumann (7.83 Hz), Alpha, Beta, Gamma, Gamma-40
• Solfeggio + natural tuning frequencies: 111, 136, 174, 256, 285, 396, 417, 432, 444, 528, 639, 741, 852, 963 Hz
• Save your own custom presets
• Layer your own background music
• Sleep timer

**Breath Work**
• 18 techniques across calming and activating styles: Box, 4-7-8, Diaphragmatic, Pursed-Lip, Coherent (5·5), Bhramari (Bee), Nadi Shodhana, Sitali, Physiological Sigh, Ujjayi (Ocean), Dirga (Three-Part), Holotropic, Shamanic, SOMA, Bhastrika, Lion's Breath, Kapalabhati, Wim Hof Style
• Two visualizations: a minimal breathing circle, or a polygonal mandala
• Mudras (hand positions) paired with each technique
• Mala counter to 108

**Chakras**
• Seven chakras with their Devanagari bija mantras (लं वं रं यं हं ॐ)
• Element, body location, qualities, and what blocks look like
• Tap to tune both ears around the chakra's carrier frequency
• Ayurvedic doshas — Vata, Pitta, Kapha — with balancing recommendations

**Horoscopes**
• Daily, monthly, and yearly horoscopes for your sign
• Lunar phase indicator everywhere
• Tarot card of the moment with grounded interpretations

**More**
• Profile with MBTI mini-quiz (16-personalities-style)
• Compatibility for two birth profiles
• AI Insights — bring your own free Gemini API key for thematic analysis of your mood + gratitude journal, and AI tarot interpretation
• Daily Affirmation card with optional gentle notifications
• Mood check-in with a 14-day graph
• Gratitude journal
• 5-4-3-2-1 grounding exercise
• First-launch onboarding flow tailored to your intent

**Privacy**
No accounts, no ads. Your mood and gratitude journal stays on your device unless you opt in to AI Insights. Anonymous crash reports go to Sentry so bugs get fixed. Network requests only happen when you actively use a feature that needs them (horoscopes, tarot, AI insights, bug reports); on the web version, horoscope and tarot requests pass through a small proxy server that stores nothing. See our privacy policy for the full breakdown.

Use stereo headphones for the binaural effect.
