## Loop lengths

Soft Rain, Trickling Stream, and Forest Air ship as three-minute loops cut
from the recordings above (starting 18:00, 4:00, and 5:00 in) rather than the
full 36-, 27-, and 10-minute files. Each loop's last four seconds are the
recording's own continuation crossfaded into its first four seconds, so the
wrap point is continuous. Segments were chosen so their mean level sits
within 0.5 dB of the whole recording; `SOUNDSCAPE_GAIN` is unchanged. This
took the bundled audio from about 60 MB to 18 MB. White Noise keeps its
original file: re-encoding broadband noise measurably dulled it.
